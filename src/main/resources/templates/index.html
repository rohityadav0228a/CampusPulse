System Workflow

How Complaints Will Be Handled

CampusPulse is being built around a clear 4-step lifecycle to ensure every student issue is acknowledged, reviewed, and resolved systematically.

01

Submission

Students select the appropriate campus category, provide a specific description of the issue, and receive a reference number upon submission.

02

Administrative Review

Campus administrators review pending submissions from their dashboard, validate urgency, and route the case to the concerned personnel.

03

Departmental Action

The designated team addresses the reported issue on the ground, updating the complaint status from Pending to In Progress.

04

Resolution & Closure

Once corrective action is taken, the complaint status is marked as Resolved with closure notes accessible via the tracking lookup.

System Features

Designed for Campus Accountability

The core architectural components being integrated into CampusPulse to provide a structured, transparent feedback system.

Centralized Grievance Registry

A single unified database schema to manage submissions from across campus hostels, academic departments, and physical facilities.

Reference ID Tracking

Planned lookup feature that allows students to check their complaint status anytime without needing to visit administrative offices.

Categorized Routing

Explicit categorization segregates complaints into departments such as Academic, Hostel, Infrastructure, and Dining for targeted review.

Administrative Dashboard

Planned staff interface to view submitted complaints, filter by category or status, and update resolution notes with audit trails.

Responsive Web Interface

Engineered with clean HTML5 and CSS to provide optimal accessibility and readability across smartphones, tablets, and desktop workstations.

Data Validation & Integrity

Spring Validation and JPA persistence ensure complaint submissions meet required standards and persist reliably in the MySQL database.

Campus Domains

Grievance Categories

Submissions are organized into specific campus domains to direct each issue to the appropriate authority for timely attention.

CAT-ACAD

Academic Affairs

Issues relating to classroom sessions, exam schedules, course materials, and department coordination.

Typical Topics:
Timetable Conflicts Course Access Evaluation
CAT-HOSTEL

Hostel & Residence

Residential living conditions including room maintenance, electricity, plumbing, water supply, and hygiene.

Typical Topics:
Room Maintenance Water Supply Sanitation
CAT-INFRA

Infrastructure & Labs

Campus facilities, computer labs, projectors, Wi-Fi networking, electrical fixtures, and building safety.

Typical Topics:
Lab Equipment Wi-Fi & Internet Lighting
CAT-LIB

Library & Study Spaces

Book catalog availability, digital library access, reading hall acoustics, and circulation desk procedures.

Typical Topics:
Book Availability Study Halls Digital Subscriptions
CAT-DINE

Canteen & Dining

Campus cafeteria cleanliness, meal serving timings, food quality standards, and drinking water facilities.

Typical Topics:
Food Hygiene Service Hours Drinking Water
CAT-TRANS

Transit & Campus Grounds

Campus shuttle connectivity, vehicular parking management, grounds maintenance, and general safety.

Typical Topics:
Bus Timing Parking Access Walkway Lights
